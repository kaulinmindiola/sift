# ADR-NNN: Title as a short noun phrase

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Phase:** F0–F8
- **Related:** requirement ids (FR-, NFR-, VR-), open decisions (D1–D12), other ADRs

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, the constraints from the SRS/SDD, and what
makes the choice non-obvious. Facts only; no decision yet.

## Decision

What we do, stated in one or two sentences, followed by the details that a
reviewer needs to check the code against it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to maintain.
Include the limitations we accept knowingly.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative with the reason it lost. "We did not think of it" is
not an alternative.

## What the implementation showed

Filled in when the phase closes (and reviewed in F8): measurements, surprises
and anything that would change the decision next time.
